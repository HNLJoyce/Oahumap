Oʻahu Enforcement Heat Map

Scheduled enforcements by DFM, DLNR and DOT

  • DFM: Department of Facility Maintenance. Maintains City roads, bridges, streams, public facilities, and other infrastructure, and conducts daily Stored Property Ordinance (SPO) and related public-space operations.
  • DLNR: Department of Land and Natural Resources. Conducts enforcement, postings, and cleanups on state lands and natural resource areas, often in coordination with partner agencies.
  • DOT: Hawaiʻi Department of Transportation. Its teams conduct enforcement and cleanups on state highways, ramps, bridges and other state transportation property, often alongside the City, HPD and DLNR.

How it works: Use the panel at top right to choose agencies and a time period. Hotter areas and larger circles show places that appear more often in the selected period. Select a circle to see its dates and crews.

Location points are based on named sites, street midpoints, intersections, or area centroids, depending on the schedule detail provided. A place listed for both the day and night crew on the same date counts once for that date. Struck-through calendar items are not plotted. DLNR calendars are marked subject to change, and items marked tentative are plotted.
