Oʻahu Enforcement Heat Map
  • DFM: Department of Facility Maintenance. Maintains City roads, bridges, streams, public facilities, and other infrastructure, and conducts daily Stored Property Ordinance (SPO) and related public-space operations.
  • DLNR: Department of Land and Natural Resources. Conducts enforcement, postings, and cleanups on state lands and natural resource areas, often in coordination with partner agencies.
  • DCS: Department of Community Services. Designs and coordinates large-scale encampment-reduction strategies with multiple interdepartmental partners, including federal, state, City and County of Honolulu, and nonprofit agencies.
How it works: Use the layer control at top right to toggle by agency and month. Hotter areas and larger circles show places that appear more often in that selected layer.
Location points are based on named sites, street midpoints, intersections, or area centroids, depending on the schedule detail provided. August includes DFM schedules through Aug. 14 and the DLNR August calendar; the DLNR calendar is marked subject to change. Non-geographic notes and locations that cannot be identified from the schedule are not plotted.
