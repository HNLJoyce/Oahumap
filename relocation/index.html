Data notes & reporting conventions
The first reporting period of May 20–June 5, 2026 includes all relocation activities for both organizations from January through then. For the month filter, that initial cumulative period is shown separately as “Jan–May baseline”; later months aggregate the weekly reporting periods by their report end month. Thereafter, IHS generally reports weekly; the latest update covers October 23–30, 2026. This version includes IHS activity through October 30, 2026. IHS reported zero activity for June 27–July 3, August 28–September 4, and September 18–25. Beginning August 14, 2026, WBID also sends updates weekly. The August 4 WBID relocation to Atlanta was recorded as a party of three: two men and their dog. WBID reported an additional Philadelphia relocation on September 12, 2026; for consistent weekly filtering, it is grouped with the September 11 reporting update (September 4–11). IHS destinations listed as “Undisclosed” are included in totals but are not plotted on the map.
